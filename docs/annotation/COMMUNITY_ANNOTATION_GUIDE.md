# Community Annotation Guide for Zolai

## Overview

This guide helps community members annotate Zolai language data for quality improvement.

## Getting Started

### 1. Access the Platform
- URL: [TBD - will be deployed]

### 2. Understand the Task
You will review Zolai-English translation pairs and:
- Rate translation quality (1-5 stars)
- Rate cultural accuracy (1-5 stars)
- Flag issues (if any)
- Suggest corrections (optional)

### 3. Annotation Guidelines

#### Translation Quality (1-5)
- **1 (Poor):** Major errors, wrong meaning
- **2 (Fair):** Some errors, partially correct
- **3 (Good):** Minor issues, mostly accurate
- **4 (Very Good):** Accurate, natural phrasing
- **5 (Excellent):** Perfect translation

#### Cultural Accuracy (1-5)
- **1 (Inappropriate):** Disrespectful or wrong context
- **2 (Questionable):** May offend or mislead
- **3 (Acceptable):** Generally appropriate
- **4 (Good):** Culturally sensitive
- **5 (Excellent):** Perfect cultural fit

#### Common Issues
- Grammar error: Wrong word order, missing markers
- Vocabulary error: Wrong word choice
- Cultural inappropriateness: Disrespectful or misleading
- Missing context: Need more information
- Unnatural phrasing: Technically correct but awkward

### 4. Tips for Good Annotation

1. **Read the full context** before rating
2. **Trust your instincts** as a native speaker
3. **Flag issues** even if you can't fix them
4. **Suggest corrections** when possible
5. **Take breaks** to maintain quality

### 5. Quality Control

- Each item is annotated by 3+ people
- Disagreements are resolved by elders
- You can see your accuracy score
- High-quality annotators get more complex tasks

### 6. Recognition

- All annotators credited in publications
- Top contributors get special recognition
- Community benefit from improved data
- Skills development opportunity

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to be a linguist?**
A: No! Native speakers are most valuable. Linguistic training helps but isn't required.

**Q: What if I'm not sure about a translation?**
A: Flag it for review. Better to mark uncertain than guess.

**Q: Can I annotate on my phone?**
A: Yes! The platform is mobile-friendly.

**Q: How much time does this take?**
A: As much or as little as you want. Even 15 minutes helps.

**Q: Who sees my annotations?**
A: Project team and community elders. Your name is credited unless you prefer anonymity.
